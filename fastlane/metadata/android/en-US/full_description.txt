Moja potrošnja struje is a simple, free and privacy-friendly application for tracking household electricity meter readings.

Enter the reading date, higher tariff (VT) and lower tariff (NT). The application displays electricity consumption between readings, consumption history, a chart and an informational cost estimate.

Features:
• recording VT and NT meter readings
• consumption calculation between reading periods
• informational electricity cost estimate
• consumption chart and reading history
• JSON backup and restore
• completely offline operation

All readings remain locally on the device. The application has no advertisements, analytics, user accounts or Internet permission.

This is not an official HEP application. Calculations are informational and may differ from the actual electricity bill.
